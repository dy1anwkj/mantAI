Additional PDFs found using the Codex built-in browser, 7 October 2026.
04 Japanese Mahjong Strategy (Puyo): 73 pages. Start with chapter 1, tile efficiency. Riichi theory and Japanese safe-tile assumptions do not apply directly to Singapore play.
05 Aspects of Mahjong Strategy: 22-page MCR slide deck. Useful exercises about kongs and chows. Its scoring is for Mahjong Competition Rules, not Singapore tai.
06 Mahjong Basics (Christopher F. Chung): 18 pages, traditional Chinese beginner introduction. Confirm Singapore scoring and bonus rules separately.
07 Singapore First Hu learner sheet and 08 answer notes: beginner exercises under Wong Hong Wei's fixed practice-table rules. Independent expert review is pending; these are not universal Singapore rules.
