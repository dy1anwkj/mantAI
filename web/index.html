Welcome to the table

Everyone starts with 1,000 points. Balances carry into the next hand. One payout unit is one point. The base for 1–5 tai is 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 points. On a discard win, the discarder pays twice the base and the other two each pay the base. On a self-draw, all three pay twice the base. Existing Kong and bonus payments also count. Restarting the server resets balances.

You play against three AI opponents. Click a tile once to discard it. Use Chi, Pong, Kong or Hu when offered, or Pass.

The first banker is you, Dong. The banker stays after winning; another winner moves the banker one seat. A draw keeps the banker.

Roll three dice. Count from the banker as 1, anticlockwise. On the selected wall, count stacks from its owner's right to left. Break after stack N and take the first two stacks to the left of the gap.

Four-tile packets go anticlockwise, starting with the banker. After three rounds, each player gets one tile and the banker one extra. Flowers, seasons and animals are exposed and replaced.
